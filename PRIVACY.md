# Privacy

This application runs locally and does not require a cloud account.

- Imported DICOM, panoramic images, measurements, and case metadata stay on the local computer.
- The application does not automatically upload patient data.
- The GitHub repository and release packages do not contain patient images or local case libraries.
- Removing the application does not automatically remove the local case library.

Operators are responsible for applying their local privacy, retention, access-control,
and medical-data protection requirements.
